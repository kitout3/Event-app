# EventApp — legal completion checklist

The public legal framework is present, but the repository does not contain verified publisher details. Before commercial launch, replace the placeholders in `public/legal.html`, then update `public/privacy.html` and `public/terms.html` with the final contractual information.

Required verified fields:
- publisher / legal entity name and legal form;
- registered office address;
- company / registration number where applicable;
- publication director;
- official support/privacy contact;
- final data retention periods by category;
- complete processor/subprocessor list and payment provider details;
- refund/cancellation terms and applicable jurisdiction;
- custom production domain, then update canonical URLs, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
